Questions

How is the score made?

Agisse lists what a posting asks for and checks each point against your CV. Below 4.0 out of 5 it calls the role a long shot and names the gaps. How the score works

Does it apply for me?

No, on purpose. Auto-apply bots flood recruiters, who then filter harder. Agisse drafts; you submit and send, from your own accounts.

Is it free, and how is it funded?

Yes, it’s free: no paid plan, no ads, and your data is never sold. Try it on free AI models. For real use, connect your own OpenRouter key and pay OpenRouter directly. The hosting costs little.

Where does my data live?

In a database in the EU. With your own AI key, requests go straight to your provider. Download your data or delete your account in Profile at any time. What goes where

Does Agisse work for employers?

No. Agisse is on your side only: it never scores or ranks candidates for employers, never sells or shares your data with them, and no employer can pay to appear in your feed.

Who’s behind it?

Lennart started Agisse to help people learn about AI while searching and applying for jobs. It is an open-source project, not a company.

Can I use it from Claude, ChatGPT or Codex?

From Claude, Codex, Cursor and other MCP clients: sign in with your Agisse account and they can read your feed and tracker. Set it up

Is it open source, and what isn’t open?

All the code is open source (AGPL). The job data on agisse.xyz isn’t: the hosted service collects and maintains it. Your own copy starts with an empty feed. Run it yourself
