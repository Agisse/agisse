Questions

Does Agisse apply or message people for me?

No, and that is on purpose. It fills forms and drafts messages; you submit and send them yourself, from your own accounts.

Who is Agisse for?

Students and graduates in their first few years of work who are looking for internships, graduate programmes and first jobs in Europe.

What does it cost?

Agisse is free during the beta. Your first search works while you wait on the waitlist.

What does it need from me?

Your CV. Import it as a PDF and check the fields it fills: your background is what the ranking and the drafts are built on.
