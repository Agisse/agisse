# Hired with Agisse

People who used Agisse and got the job. Each row links to an issue that person opened from their own GitHub account.

Got the job? [Share your story](https://github.com/Agisse/agisse/issues/new?template=hired.yml). You choose what shows here:

- **Handle and role:** your GitHub handle and the role.
- **Role only:** the role, no name.
- **Count only:** one more on the count, nothing else.

The issue itself stays public under your account, whichever you choose.

To withdraw your story, comment "withdraw" on your issue. Its row then reads "Story withdrawn" and still counts: the number never goes down.

| # | Who | Role | Story |
|---|---|---|---|

Maintainers: add one row per `hired` issue, in the order they were opened, showing only what the person chose, and update the count in `README.md` in the same commit. Never write a row for someone else.
