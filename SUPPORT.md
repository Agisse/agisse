# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas**: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/agisse/agisse/discussions).
- **Bugs**: open an issue with steps to reproduce.
- **Using agisse.xyz** (your account or your data): hello@agisse.xyz.
- **Security**: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Self-hosting

Two ways are supported: on your computer (`npx github:Agisse/agisse`) and on Cloudflare (`npm run self-host`). [docs/self-host.md](docs/self-host.md) covers both.

Help comes from the community in Discussions, on a best-effort basis, with no response times. Other targets, such as Docker, Kubernetes or other clouds, aren't supported: each one is more to maintain, so a new one needs the maintainer's decision first.
