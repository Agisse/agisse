# Why Agisse exists

I started Agisse to help people learn how to use AI while searching and applying for jobs. The name is a reminder to take action yourself.

Agisse tells you where to put your effort, and then gets out of the way.

## The name

*Agisse* is French: « il faut que j'agisse », "I must act". You say it ah-ZHEESS.

Every part of Agisse should end in one next step you take yourself: apply, reach out, prepare.

## What Agisse will never do

- **Apply for you.** No auto-apply, no auto-submit. You press send.
- **Message people for you.** It drafts the note; you send it. It never logs into LinkedIn.
- **Make things up about you.** A tailored CV uses only what's in yours. What it can't know stays a blank for you to fill.
- **Sell your data or show ads.** Your data never becomes the product.
- **Sell places in the feed.** Roles are ranked against your profile, never for money. [How the score works](/methodology).

## How agisse.xyz stays free

Agisse is free and open source. There's no company behind it, no paid plan and no donations.

- **Trying it costs nothing.** Agisse's shared AI runs on its own key, with a small daily limit per account.
- **For real use, connect your own OpenRouter key.** Better models, your own account, and you pay OpenRouter directly.
- **Hosting is cheap.** It runs on Cloudflare's and Neon's free tiers; the shared AI on Agisse's key is Mistral (EU), paid with a daily cap. [What it costs to run](/open).

## What's open

All of Agisse's code is open source under the AGPL. You can read it, check what it does with your data, and run it yourself with `npx agisse`.

The job data on agisse.xyz is not open: the hosted service collects and maintains it. A copy you run yourself starts with an empty feed. [Self-hosting](/self-host).
