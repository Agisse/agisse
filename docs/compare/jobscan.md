# Agisse and Jobscan

*Checked 14 October 2026 against [jobscan.co](https://www.jobscan.co).*

[Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co) compares your resume with a job listing (hard skills, soft skills, keywords) and reports how to tailor it for applicant tracking systems. It also has a resume builder, a cover letter generator, LinkedIn profile optimisation, a job tracker and Auto Apply, where you review each application before it's sent.

| | Agisse | Jobscan |
|---|---|---|
| Finding roles | A feed of European roles ranked against your CV | Jobs matched to your resume |
| Verdict | 1–5 score; below 4.0 says don't apply | A match report against the listing |
| Applying | Tailored CV, cover letter and drafted answers to copy; you submit | Resume and cover letter help, and Auto Apply after your review |
| People | Warm intros from shared ground (university, employer, student organisation); you send the note | Not on its homepage |
| Prep | Interview and online-assessment practice | Not on its homepage |
| Tracker | Yes | Yes |
| Price | Free | A free resume scan; paid plans |
| Code | Open source (AGPL-3.0); you can self-host it | Closed |

**Jobscan fits you if** your main worry is getting your resume past applicant tracking systems.

**Agisse fits you if** you're looking in Europe and want to know first whether a role is worth applying to, then send fewer, better applications yourself.
