# Agisse and Teal

*Checked 14 October 2026 against [tealhq.com](https://www.tealhq.com) and [Teal's pricing](https://www.tealhq.com/pricing).*

[Teal](https://www.tealhq.com) is a resume builder and job tracker. Its free Chrome extension saves jobs from over 40 job boards into the tracker, and it matches your resume's keywords against a job description.

| | Agisse | Teal |
|---|---|---|
| Finding roles | A feed of European roles ranked against your CV | Save jobs from other boards with its extension; a tech job board |
| Verdict | 1–5 score; below 4.0 says don't apply | A match score and keyword insights for your resume |
| Applying | Tailored CV, cover letter and drafted answers to copy; you submit | Tailored resumes, AI bullets, summaries and cover letters; you apply |
| People | Warm intros from shared ground (university, employer, student organisation); you send the note | Email templates per job stage |
| Prep | Interview and online-assessment practice | Interview practice and salary negotiation tools |
| Tracker | Yes | Yes |
| Price | Free | Free plan; Teal+ at $13 a week |
| Code | Open source (AGPL-3.0); you can self-host it | Closed |

**Teal fits you if** you mainly want a strong resume builder and one place to track jobs you find elsewhere.

**Agisse fits you if** you're looking in Europe and want the roles found and ranked for you, a straight "don't apply" when one doesn't fit, and introductions to people you share ground with.
