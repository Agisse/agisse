# Agisse and Simplify

*Checked 14 October 2026 against [simplify.jobs](https://simplify.jobs) and Simplify's [GitHub lists](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs).*

[Simplify](https://simplify.jobs) is a job search platform: it matches roles to your profile, tailors your resume, fills in application forms with its Copilot browser extension, and can apply for you. It also keeps free internship and new-grad lists on GitHub, updated with Pitt CSC.

| | Agisse | Simplify |
|---|---|---|
| Finding roles | A feed of European roles ranked against your CV | Postings indexed daily from 50+ job boards and career pages; free GitHub lists of tech internships and new-grad roles |
| Verdict | 1–5 score; below 4.0 says don't apply | A match score and the keywords your resume misses |
| Applying | Tailored CV, cover letter and drafted answers to copy; you submit | Resume tailoring, form autofill, and Auto Apply |
| People | Warm intros from shared ground (university, employer, student organisation); you send the note | Shows people you know at the company |
| Prep | Interview and online-assessment practice | Not on its homepage |
| Tracker | Yes | Yes, filled from what you send |
| Price | Free | Free; a paid Simplify+ subscription |
| How it's funded | Low hosting costs, paid by the project; no ads, no paid placement, no data sales | Simplify+ and employers who hire through Simplify |
| Code | Open source (AGPL-3.0); you can self-host it | Closed |

**Simplify fits you if** you want to send many applications fast, and its lists cover your field.

**Agisse fits you if** you'd rather send fewer, better applications in Europe, want a straight "don't apply" when a role doesn't fit, and want to send every application and message yourself.
