# Agisse and Huntr

*Checked 14 October 2026 against [Huntr's pricing](https://huntr.co/pricing).*

[Huntr](https://huntr.co) is a job tracker with a resume builder, AI cover letters, a resume keyword scanner, a contact tracker and application autofill through its Chrome extension.

| | Agisse | Huntr |
|---|---|---|
| Finding roles | A feed of European roles ranked against your CV | Clip jobs from other sites with its Chrome extension |
| Verdict | 1–5 score; below 4.0 says don't apply | Resume job matching and scoring |
| Applying | Tailored CV, cover letter and drafted answers to copy; you submit | Tailored resumes, AI cover letters and form autofill |
| People | Warm intros from shared ground (university, employer, student organisation); you send the note | A contact tracker |
| Prep | Interview and online-assessment practice | An interview tracker |
| Tracker | Yes | Yes, up to 100 jobs on the free plan |
| Price | Free | Free plan; Pro at $40 a month, less when paid for 3 or 6 months |
| Code | Open source (AGPL-3.0); you can self-host it | Closed |

**Huntr fits you if** you want a polished tracker for a search you run yourself, with autofill.

**Agisse fits you if** you're looking in Europe and want the roles found and ranked for you, a straight "don't apply" when one doesn't fit, and practice for the interviews and tests.
