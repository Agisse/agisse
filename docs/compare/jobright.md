# Agisse and Jobright

*Checked 14 October 2026 against [jobright.ai](https://jobright.ai) and Jobright's post [Is Jobright legit?](https://jobright.ai/blog/is-jobright-legit/).*

[Jobright](https://jobright.ai) is an AI job search copilot: job matches, one-click application autofill, tailored resumes, insider connections and an AI career assistant. Its homepage search starts in the United States and it has an H1B jobs section.

| | Agisse | Jobright |
|---|---|---|
| Finding roles | A feed of European roles ranked against your CV | Matches against your skills, from a job hub it says holds 8,000,000+ jobs |
| Verdict | 1–5 score; below 4.0 says don't apply | Matches "you're truly qualified for" |
| Applying | Tailored CV, cover letter and drafted answers to copy; you submit | Tailored resume and one-click autofill "across all major ATS platforms" |
| People | Warm intros from shared ground (university, employer, student organisation); you send the note | Alumni and hiring managers as insider referrals |
| Prep | Interview and online-assessment practice | Interview questions and its AI assistant, Orion |
| Price | Free | Free basic plan; Premium at $30 a month |
| How it's funded | Low hosting costs, paid by the project; no ads, no paid placement, no data sales | Premium plans; venture-backed |
| Code | Open source (AGPL-3.0); you can self-host it | Closed |

**Jobright fits you if** you're looking in the United States and want applications filled in for you.

**Agisse fits you if** you're looking in Europe, want a straight "don't apply" when a role doesn't fit, and want to send every application and message yourself.
