# Agisse and LinkedIn Easy Apply

*Checked 14 October 2026 against LinkedIn Help, [Apply to jobs directly on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a512348).*

[Easy Apply](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a512348) lets you apply to many LinkedIn listings without leaving LinkedIn. It can save your resume and answers, pre-fill them next time, and include skills from your profile. LinkedIn sets daily and speed limits on it, to keep applications thoughtful.

They do different jobs, and work together: Agisse helps you decide where to apply and prepares what you send; Easy Apply is one way to send it. Agisse never logs into LinkedIn or applies for you.

| | Agisse | LinkedIn Easy Apply |
|---|---|---|
| Finding roles | A feed of European roles ranked against your CV | LinkedIn's job search, with recommendations from your saved information |
| Verdict | 1–5 score; below 4.0 says don't apply | None |
| Applying | Tailored CV, cover letter and drafted answers to copy; you submit | Your saved resume and answers, sent from LinkedIn |
| People | Warm intros from shared ground (university, employer, student organisation); you send the note | Your LinkedIn network |
| Prep | Interview and online-assessment practice | Not part of Easy Apply |
| Price | Free | Free with a LinkedIn account |
| Code | Open source (AGPL-3.0); you can self-host it | Closed |

**Use Easy Apply** when a role you've chosen offers it and your tailored CV is ready.

**Use Agisse first** to find out whether the role is worth it, and to tailor what you send.
