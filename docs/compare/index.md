# Compare

One page per tool, written from that tool's own public pages and dated. Features and prices change: if a page is out of date, [open an issue](https://github.com/Agisse/agisse/issues/new) and we'll fix it.

What Agisse is, for the comparison: a free, open-source (AGPL-3.0) job search copilot for students and early-career people in Europe. It ranks European roles against your CV, says plainly when a role isn't worth applying to (below 4.0 out of 5), finds people you share real ground with, and helps you prepare. It never applies or sends a message for you.

- [career-ops](/compare/career-ops): open-source agent that runs in an AI coding CLI
- [Simplify](/compare/simplify): job platform with autofill and auto-apply
- [Jobright](/compare/jobright): AI job search copilot with autofill
