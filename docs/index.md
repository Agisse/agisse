# Agisse

Agisse is an AI job search assistant for students and early-career job seekers in Europe. It ranks open roles against your CV, tells you which to apply to and which to skip, tailors your CV and cover letter, finds the people worth messaging at a company and drafts the note, and prepares you for each interview round. It never applies or sends messages for you, and never logs into LinkedIn. [Request an invite at agisse.xyz](https://agisse.xyz).

Two ways to connect to it beyond the web app:

- **[Browser extension](/extension)** — send a LinkedIn profile or job posting into Agisse with one click.
- **[MCP](/mcp)** — connect an AI client (Claude Desktop, Claude Code, ...) to your account.
- **[Architecture](/architecture)**: how Agisse is built, from the Worker to the database and the AI providers.
- **[Selection processes and online tests](/guides/)** — what McKinsey, BCG, Goldman Sachs, the Big Four and others ask of graduates and interns, and every test explained.
